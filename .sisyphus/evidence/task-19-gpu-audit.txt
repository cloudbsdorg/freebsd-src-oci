Verdict (one line):
  DRI is the right abstraction; DRM2 hooks available at open/mmap/ioctl,
  but the actual KMS drivers (i915kms, amdgpu, radeonkms, nvidia.ko)
  are NOT in this base tree.

  - devfs_rule_add()     → DEVFSIO_RADD ioctl on devfs mount fd
