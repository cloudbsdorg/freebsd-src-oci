15. Verdict
==============================================================================
bhyvegc is pure pixel manipulation, safe for non-VM use.
0 hidden couplings.

The only VMM dependency in the whole pipeline is one line in pci_fbuf.c:
	sc->fb_base = vm_create_devmem(pi->pi_vmctx, VM_FRAMEBUFFER,
	    "framebuffer", FB_SIZE);
and even that is hidden behind a void * before it reaches bhyvegc.

Function table (13 entries, all non-VMM-coupled):

  console_init, console_set_fbaddr, console_get_image
  console_fb_register, console_refresh
  console_kbd_register, console_key_event
  console_ptr_register, console_ptr_event
